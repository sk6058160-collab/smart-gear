# Smart Gear 🚗⚙️ – Drive smarter in NYC

**A map-first shift companion for NYC rideshare drivers (v3.5).** It shows where rides are likely right now, how long you'd wait and how far away each spot is, navigates you there, tracks your online time and GPS miles, and can start your shift automatically when your phone connects to your car. All data stays on your phone. No account needed, and everything except map tiles works offline.

## Features
- **Home (Uber-driver style):** clean white design with a full-screen light OpenStreetMap map, your blue location dot and busy spots. Primary buttons are blue (GO, Navigate, Search, active chips and tabs); STOP and End navigation are red; the Smart Gear logo stays black. A status pill at the top center shows **Offline** or **Online 1h 23m**. The round buttons are Settings (top left), Search (top right) and My location. The big blue **GO** button starts your shift (it pulses gently while you're offline) and becomes a red stop button while you're online. The bottom sheet shows "You're offline / You're online", today's online time, the **best spot** (lowest drive time + expected wait), and **Search** / **Where to go** buttons. Drag the sheet up to see a **Top 5 busy right now** list (borough, level, estimated wait, drive time and a Navigate button), today's and this week's online time, GPS miles, shift counts and recent shifts.
- **⬢ Demand view:** tap the **Demand** chip on the home map to show an Uber Driver-style hexagon heatmap over NYC (red = very busy, amber = busy, light gray = slow). A time slider (**Now, +1h, +2h, +3h**) previews predicted demand. Labeled *Estimated demand — not live Uber data*. The hex grid is capped and only redraws when you stop moving the map, so it stays smooth on phones.
- **⏱️ Wait & drive-time estimates:** small pills on the maps show the estimated wait for your next request (from the busy score, e.g. "~5–10 min") and the drive time from your GPS location ("12 min away": straight-line distance × 1.3 ÷ NYC average speed of ~12 mph at rush hour, ~18 mph off-peak, ~22 mph late night). Only the top spots get pills; zoomed out they collapse to dots. Scanner rows show the same estimates.
- **🧭 Built-in navigation:** **Navigate** (on map popups, Scanner rows, the Top 5 list and the best-spot card) opens a full-screen turn-by-turn view: route line, next-turn banner, arrival time, minutes and miles left, auto-follow with optional heading-up, automatic reroute when you go off route (at most once every 30 seconds), optional voice prompts, and an **End** button. Routing uses the public OSRM demo server with OpenStreetMap data. It's basic and has **no live traffic**. If you're offline or a route can't be found, a small **Open in Google Maps** fallback link appears. **v3.3:** your position is a blue navigation arrow (also on the home map while you're online) that points in your direction of travel (from GPS heading, or from your last positions, ignoring GPS jitter under ~3 m). The arrow glides smoothly between GPS fixes, turns the short way, and snaps to the route line when you're within ~25 m. The camera pans smoothly, zooms in closer when you're slow and out on fast roads, and has a **Heading up** option that rotates the map. The part of the route you've driven turns gray, the road ahead stays blue, and the turn banner shows a lane-style maneuver arrow plus lane guidance when the route data includes it. After you pan the map, **Re-center** brings the camera back.
- **🧭 v3.5 navigation look:** while navigating, the map switches to a dark night style (standard OpenStreetMap tiles shown dark, so buildings, house numbers and street names stay readable when zoomed in close), with a black top banner (turn arrow, distance and a big street name), a dark puck with a white ring and arrow that turns with you in heading-up mode and sits in the lower third of the screen, a street-name chip under the puck, and a black bottom bar with minutes, miles left, arrival time, "Navigating to …", a turn list and a settings button (Voice, Heading up / North up). A **speed-limit sign** appears only when OpenStreetMap has a posted limit (maxspeed in mph) for your street; otherwise the badge shows your current GPS speed in mph – limits are never guessed. Re-center sits on the right. All other screens keep the white theme with blue buttons.
- **⚡ Faster rerouting (v3.5):** high-accuracy GPS with fresh fixes; fixes worse than ±50 m are ignored; you're off route when you're more than 25 m (or the GPS accuracy) from the line on 2 fixes in a row, for about 3 seconds, or when you're clearly heading the wrong way. The new route starts from where you are and the direction you're driving, at most once every 8 seconds; the banner shows **Rerouting…** and the old route stays until the new one arrives.
- **📴 Offline & GPS loss (v3.5):** when a route starts, its line, turn-by-turn steps and destination are saved on your phone. If the internet drops, guidance continues from the saved steps ("Offline – following saved route", no rerouting), and an active route resumes after the app is reopened. Map tiles you've already looked at are kept for offline use (up to 7 days / ~800 tiles). Smart Gear does **not** download tiles ahead of time along the route, because OpenStreetMap's tile policy forbids prefetching. If GPS drops out, the puck keeps moving along the route at your last speed for up to 30 seconds ("GPS signal lost"), then holds until the next good fix.
- **👆 Touch (v3.5):** double-tap and pinch zoom are turned off for the app's screens, so taps never zoom the page. Pinch-to-zoom works on the maps only. **Pull to refresh** on the Scanner lists (Where to go, Airport incl. Airport queues, Long rides, My areas), the Top 5 list, search results and the offline map list: pull down at the top to update estimates for the current time, your GPS position and drive times, and re-run your search ("Pull to refresh" → "Release to refresh" → "Updated 1:12 PM").
- **🔍 Search (v3.4, replaces Log pickup):** the blue **Search** button (or the magnifier at the top right) opens a search bar for NYC places and addresses. Your 51 saved busy areas match instantly as you type; addresses and places come from OpenStreetMap's free **Nominatim** geocoder, biased to the NYC area (requests wait until you pause typing for ~0.4 s, at most one per second, and results are cached). Each result shows the name, borough, estimated busy level and drive time. Tap one to drop a pin on the map with a **Navigate** button (built-in navigation). Offline, only your saved areas are searched. Pickups you logged in older versions stay on your phone and still quietly improve the estimates, but there is no longer a pickup-logging screen.
- **✈️ Airports are info only (v3.4):** Smart Gear never recommends JFK, LGA or EWR (or their waiting lots) in Where to go, the best spot, Top 5, the Demand heatmap or Long rides, because airport pickups run through each app's own FHV queue. Airports show as ✈️ info markers; tap one for an estimated airport demand level, the waiting lots and a link to the official source. **Scanner → Airport → Airport queues** lists, per airport, the rideshare / FHV waiting lots (JFK Cell Phone Lot on 130th Place, Limo Lot at Cargo Plaza, FHV Hold Lot 3 at Howard Beach for Terminal 5 & 7; LGA FHV Hold Lot at 46-10 Berrian Blvd and Cell Phone Lot on 94th St; EWR Rideshare Hold Lot on Earhart Drive), the main rules, an estimated demand level from typical arrival times, and links to PANYNJ, Uber and Lyft driver pages (last checked 09/30/2026). The Busy Map also marks the lots with **P**. **Queue length and wait time are not publicly available** for rideshare lots – your Uber or Lyft driver app shows your place in line. (The Port Authority's live dashboard covers yellow-taxi lots only and isn't shown in the app.)
- **Ride Scanner (estimates):** 51 NYC-area hotspots come preloaded across Manhattan, Brooklyn, Queens, the Bronx and Staten Island, plus Newark (EWR) and Yonkers. They include casinos (Resorts World Casino NYC, Empire City Casino by MGM), restaurant districts (Koreatown, Little Italy / Chinatown, West Village, Flushing, Arthur Ave, Smith St) and clubs / nightlife (Meatpacking, Hell's Kitchen, East Village, Williamsburg, Bushwick, Lower East Side). You can filter by borough.
  - **🔥 Where to go:** the best areas right now, or at any time you pick.
  - **✈️ Airport:** Airport queues (waiting lots, rules, estimated demand, official links) plus where you're most likely to get a ride *to* JFK / LGA / EWR.
  - **🛣️ Long rides:** where long trips (suburbs, NJ, Long Island, Westchester) are most likely. Casinos are marked long-ride friendly.
  - **📍 My areas:** add, edit or delete areas (with GPS), or restore the NYC defaults.
- **🗺️ Busy Map:** light OpenStreetMap map with colored busy spots (**red = Very busy, amber = Busy, light gray with a dark outline = Slow**), five-borough shading, wait/drive-time pills and an optional **Demand hexes** layer. It has layers (Where to go / Airport / Long rides), type filters (Casinos, Restaurants, Clubs & nightlife, Airports & transit, Events & hotels), popups with wait, drive time, distance and **Navigate**, and an offline list fallback.
- **GPS:** auto-tracks shift miles, finds the nearest busy area and gives drive times.
- **Online / offline badge:** the app tells you when you're offline and keeps working.
- **Bluetooth auto-shift (Android APK only):** choose your car's Bluetooth in Settings. Your shift starts when the phone connects and pauses when it disconnects.
- **Settings:** city for Maps search, long-ride threshold (miles / minutes), GPS miles, car Bluetooth, and delete all data. (The app uses a single white theme.)

> Ride Scanner and Busy Map scores, wait times, drive times and the demand heatmap are **estimates** based on general time-of-day / day-of-week patterns (and pickups logged in older versions). They are not real Uber or Lyft demand data. Hotspot locations are approximate. Smart Gear is not affiliated with Uber, Lyft, Revel, MGM, Resorts World or NYC TLC. Map data: © OpenStreetMap contributors. Routing: OSRM demo server (OpenStreetMap data). Search: Nominatim (© OpenStreetMap contributors). Speed limits: OpenStreetMap maxspeed via the Overpass API. Airport lot info: public Uber, Lyft and PANYNJ / airport pages – rules change often, so always follow airport signs and your driver app.

## Install on iPhone (Home Screen app)
1. Open the link above in **Safari** on your iPhone.
2. Tap the **Share** button (the square with an arrow ⬆️).
3. Choose **Add to Home Screen**, then tap **Add**.
4. Open **Smart Gear** from your Home Screen.

(Bluetooth auto-shift isn't available on iPhone. Start your shift with the GO button.)

## Android
- **Download the APK:** https://sk6058160-collab.github.io/smart-gear/smart-gear.apk
  (This is a debug-signed APK. You'll need to allow "Install unknown apps" when installing it.)
- Or open the web link in Chrome, then tap ⋮ menu → **Add to Home screen / Install app**.
